CHAPTER 1

Quick start

Part 2 runs three copies of the geometric engine, one for each system around Sgr A* (I: the black hole's domain, II: the nuclear region, III: the Galaxy). On every pulse it compares the three copies exactly. Everything they fail to account for collects in one place, the emergent fourth, drawn as a crystal at the centre.

  1. Open the page and wait two seconds. Three.js loads, both 3D views start, and the verification runs on its own. The log ends with 19 checks PASS, 0 FAIL; 66/66 lines identical, and the badge next to the log title turns green.
  2. Read the badge in the crystal's top-left corner. At start it says LOCKED · Y_R = 0, H = 1. With no noise and no signal the model accounts for every measurement exactly, and the frame legs close exactly.
  3. Look at Panel C's first table. All three rows say LOCK with \(\chi = 0.577350 = 1/\sqrt3\). Each class's three nodes, one per engine, make an exactly equilateral triangle.
  4. Move Noise to 1σ. The badge changes to BREATHING, the triangles deform (\(\chi\) drops just below \(1/\sqrt3\)), the trefoils' streams pull together and the core drifts off centre. The residuals are inside the tolerance that noise allows.
  5. Press Inject error. One of the four measured frame legs is perturbed. The badge changes to ACTIVE, the holonomy row says FAIL with the closure angle, and the three trefoils shear against each other. Press it again (it now reads Clear error) and set Noise back to 0 to return to the lock.
In one sentence

When all three engines tell the same story, the triangle is equilateral and the crystal sits still. When they disagree, the triangle deforms and the crystal moves by an exactly invertible amount, so you can read the disagreement back off it.

New to the series? Part 1 builds the three systems and the residual from one engine. Its ideas (residual parity, frame closure, the tiles) are reused here without change. The companion guide explains both parts lemma by lemma.

Two kinds of σ

The page uses σ for two different things. σ the rotation is the 120° turn \((x,y,z)\mapsto(z,x,y)\) that relates the three engines. σ the uncertainty is the 1-sigma error per class (0.5 µas, 0.05 ×10⁻¹⁸ s⁻¹, 2 km/s) used by Noise and by the residuals "in units of σ". Context always tells them apart.

How it responds

  • A class residual (\(Y_R/\sigma = r\)) shifts that pair along its own 120° direction by \(0.25\tanh(r/3)\) cubits and pulls its two streams together. They merge fully at \(|r| = 5\).
  • The sum \(s = a + b + c\) of the three residuals lifts the poles along the axis by \(0.25\tanh(s/3)\).
  • The core moves to \((z \text{ in the plane} + s \text{ on the axis})/3\), through a saturating scale that keeps it inside the cube.
  • A frame error (open closure failing) twists the three trefoils against each other about the axis, by up to 0.7 rad.
  • All of these ease toward their targets (12% per frame), so the crystal glides rather than jumps.

The readouts

  • State badge (top left): LOCKED, BREATHING or ACTIVE (Chapter 12).
  • Step readout (bottom right): step k = 3 · spinor 2i · sign +. The spinor is \((1+i)^k\) reduced to its direction, "window" marks steps 2 and 6, and the sign is the spinor's sign over the 720° turn (it flips at frames 180 and 540).
  • STEP 000/720 bar: the outer-gear frame. Click anywhere on the bar to seek within the current epoch. The particle flow advances at most one step per display frame, so the streams never strobe at high speed, while the counter and the spinor turn follow the gear exactly.

16 Einstein Tiles and the Merkle root

Each update packs 16 values into 64-bit tiles (monograph §6.1). The first three rows hold the twelve streams: astrometry, timing and velocity for System I, II, III and R. The fourth row holds the four holonomy projections \(w, x, y, z\). A ≈ mark means the 32-bit float in the tile is not exactly the rational it stands for, which is why each leaf also carries the exact value.

The line below gives the root \(\tau_1\) of the SHA-256 chain over all 16 leaves, the current step and its phase (onload at step 2, offload at step 6, third otherwise), and the South Halo bytes. The South Halo is a quick pre-filter, not an integrity check.

Tamper with one leaf adds \(2^{-24}\) to one random value and re-hashes. The root always changes. The tile word may not, because a float can be too coarse to show the change, and the South Halo never does. That is why the chain hashes the exact value.

CHAPTER 12

Locked, breathing, active

The soliton has three states. They are decided exactly, from Layer B's rationals.

StateConditionWhat you see
LOCKEDEvery class residual \(Y_R\) is exactly 0 and the measured legs close exactlyGold core at the centre, gold cube, streams at full separation, interior cells (1, 0, 0, 0), no web in Panel A
BREATHINGNot locked, but every residual and the closure are within toleranceAmber core and cube, the core drifts, the residual cells turn amber
ACTIVEAny residual or the closure is past toleranceRed core and cube, red cells, shear if the legs fail

The tolerances scale with the Noise slider DEFINITION:

$$|Y_R| \le 3\,\sigma_c \cdot \text{Noise}\cdot\sqrt3 \quad\text{(per class)}, \qquad \text{closure angle} \le 5 \cdot \text{Noise} \cdot 0.1\ \text{mas}.$$

So with Noise at 0 the tolerance is 0, and anything non-zero is ACTIVE. That is why an injected signal or error goes straight to ACTIVE, while noise alone stays BREATHING.

Lock of the triangle vs. lock of the soliton

They are different tests. A triangle's LOCK in Panel C asks whether the three engines' phases agree for that class. The soliton's LOCKED asks whether every residual is zero and the legs close. With Coupling κ = 0 the triangles stay locked while the soliton breathes. With an injected frame error the triangles stay locked while the soliton is active.

CHAPTER 13

The lock, by hand

This lab rebuilds one class's triangle from the page's own formulas, in floating point, so you can see how Δφ deforms it. The page does the same in exact rationals.

Each engine \(s\) places its node on tier \(n\) at

$$\theta_k = \frac{2\pi k}{30}, \qquad \varphi_s = W_n\,\theta_k\,(-1)^k + \frac{2\pi c}{3} + \Delta\varphi_s, \qquad \Delta\varphi_s = \kappa\,\frac{\pi}{6}\tanh\frac{r_s}{3},$$

then applies its tier's Lemma 4 frame and the rotation \(\sigma^s\). If the three \(\Delta\varphi_s\) agree, the three nodes are \(P, \sigma P, \sigma^2 P\) and the triangle is exactly equilateral.

Cross-engine triangle

Try equal residuals: all three engines turn by the same Δφ, so the triangle stays equilateral even though every residual is 2σ. Tension and torsion act the same way in all three engines, so they never break the lock either.

CHAPTER 14

Experiments

Each recipe starts from a fresh page (reload, or set every control back to its default). The outcomes below were recorded on the J1745-2900 sightline at epoch 0 with the page paused. Noise values change from epoch to epoch, so the exact numbers will differ at other epochs, but the pattern will not.

1 · The baseline lock

Do nothing. The badge reads LOCKED. All three TQF rows read LOCK with χ = 0.577350 and Δφ = 0.0 · 0.0 · 0.0. The gauge is (0, 0, 0) with recovery exact, the measured legs read PASS exact, and Panel D shows 19 checks passing.

2 · Noise

  1. Set Noise to 1σ. The badge changes to BREATHING. All three classes deform slightly (χ ≈ 0.57731, 0.57732, 0.57723). The Δφ column shows offsets of a few hundred mrad that differ between engines. The gauge reads about (−0.88, −1.62, 0.72) and the legs read within tolerance (about 161 µas).
  2. Raise it to 3σ. The residuals triple (−2.65, −4.87, 2.17) but so does the tolerance, so the badge stays BREATHING. The streams of the timing pair nearly merge, since |b| approaches 5.

3 · Coupling off

With Noise at 3σ, set Coupling κ to 0. All three triangles snap back to LOCK (Δφ = 0) while the badge stays BREATHING and the gauge keeps its values. The triangles show the phase coupling; the soliton shows the residuals.

4 · Tension and torsion

Back at the defaults, set Tension to 40° and Torsion to 15°. Panel A's tiers tilt and the triangles change shape on screen, but Panel C still reads LOCK with χ = 0.577350: the turn is the same in all three engines. The π/6 gate reads closed (Δθ = 25°). Set Torsion to 10° and it opens (Δθ = 30°), and nothing else changes.

5 · A lensing event

  1. Choose Lensing event. The log notes the injection and the closest approach about 3.57 years away. At first nothing changes: the event starts at its edge, where its signal is zero by construction.
  2. Press Epoch ▶ fourteen times (to about 2030.3). The badge reads ACTIVE. Astrometry and timing are deformed, with Δφ = −523.6 · 0.0 · 0.0: System I's phase saturates at −π/6 while II and III stay at 0. Velocity stays LOCK. The gauge's timing residual is millions of σ, so the core sits at its saturated offset.

6 · A Shapiro event

Choose Shapiro event and step fourteen epochs. Now only timing deforms. Astrometry and velocity stay locked, because a Shapiro delay has no astrometric term.

7 · The scalar field and its range

  1. Choose Scalar field on J1745-2900 (0.10 pc). Timing and velocity deform, with Δφ non-zero in Systems I and II and zero in III. Astrometry stays locked. The badge reads ACTIVE.
  2. Switch the sightline to J1746-2850 (27.2 pc). Everything locks again. The Yukawa factor \(e^{-r/\lambda}\) with λ = 1 pc is about \(10^{-12}\) there, far below the 2⁻²⁴ grid Layer B uses, so the residual is exactly zero. The test can only see a short-range field on sightlines inside its range.

8 · A frame error

Press Inject error. The badge reads ACTIVE, and the measured legs read FAIL with an angle (the leg and the size are random: one run gave 12.15°). The four interior cells turn red, and the trefoils shear. All three triangles stay LOCK and the gauge stays at 0, because a frame error moves no node and adds no residual. Panel D's Lemma 12 row still passes: catching the error is the expected result. Press Clear error.

9 · Erasure

In the triple-quad grid, click one cell in each row: three cells show ↺ with ✓ and every group reads rebuilt. Click a second cell in one row: both cells in that row read lost. Press Restore all.

10 · Tampering

Press Tamper with one leaf a few times. The root changes every time. The South Halo never changes, and the tile word sometimes does not either, when \(2^{-24}\) is below the float's resolution at that value.

11 · Freeing the crystal

Untick Lemma 8.2 geometry and set Torus radius to 25 (the crystal file's own value). The lobes leave the rays. Panel D's Lemma 8 Step 6 row reads free with the offset (about 4.8 units), and the summary drops to 18 live checks. Tick it again to snap back.

12 · Windows and the spinor

Pause and click the STEP bar at about one-sixth of its length (frames 90–179). The readout says step k = 2 · spinor i · window. At about three-quarters (frames 450–539) it says step k = 6 · spinor −i · window, with the sign now −: the same orientation, the opposite spinor. Tick Spinor turn to watch the crystal make its 720° turn per epoch.

CHAPTER 17

Glossary

Layer A / Layer B
Layer A is the floating-point physics. Layer B is the exact rational engine: values cross at a 2⁻²⁴ grid and from then on every operation is exact BigInt.
Quadrance Q
A squared distance, the rational-trigonometry replacement for length.
Quadrea AR
Sixteen times the squared area of a triangle, computed from its three quadrances.
χ
\(\sqrt{A_R}/(Q_1+Q_2+Q_3)\). Never above \(1/\sqrt3\), and equal to it only for an equilateral triangle.
Lock (triangle)
\(3A_R = S^2\) exactly: the three engines agree for that class.
LOCKED (soliton)
Every residual is zero and the measured frame legs close exactly.
Residual YR
The measured total minus the three systems' models: the emergent fourth.
Δφ, κ
A node's phase offset, driven by its system's own residual. κ is the coupling strength.
P₄, P₄*
The inferred fourth vertex of a class's triangle (heuristic scale), and its exact closure \(P_{\text{II}} + P_{\text{III}} - P_{\text{I}}\).
Gauge (s, z)
Proposition 7.14's exact, invertible split of the three residuals into an axial part \(s\) and a planar part \(z\).
Open / cyclic closure
Open multiplies four independently measured legs and can fail. Cyclic computes the fourth from the other three and always closes, so it tests nothing (Lemma 12).
ω
The Hurwitz unit \((1+i+j+k)/2\): the 120° turn between engines as a quaternion. ω³ = −1.
Einstein Tile
A 64-bit word (§6.1) carrying a step, wheel sector, structure, gate, index, form, slot, scale and a 32-bit core.
South Halo
The tile's top byte, a quick pre-filter. It is not an integrity check; the Merkle root is.
Merkle root τ₁
The head of the SHA-256 chain over all 16 leaves, each the tile word plus its exact value (Lemma 15).
Pulse, stage, epoch, step
The clock's units (Chapter 5).
Cubit
The engine's length unit: tier radii are in cubits, and the crystal uses 20 display units per cubit.
