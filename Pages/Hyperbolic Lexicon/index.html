The six answers

The questions were: what have we made; what would SHD-CCP integration look like; how would a hyperbolic BERT work for our 2D and 3D systems; what are the alternatives; could language pre-processing data grow as SHD-CCP biochains; and which benchmarks would help. Each answer has its own explainer.

1 · What we built is exact geometry, not language learning

Exact quaternion addresses, an exact H³ tree to depth 28, hyperboloid tools, Merkle chains, the fence and a parity method. Before this system, no tokenizer, vocabulary, embeddings, encoder or language benchmark existed in any of the four repositories. engram/1 keeps only letter-count parity, so it cannot be a token id (L2: 52.6% of WordNet’s lemma types share a key); PolyStep cannot train an encoder.

Explainer 1

2 · SHD-CCP: three layers, one bridge

A specification repository, the working Blackwell word and shdccp/2 stream, and prose that was never built. At least eight 64-bit layouts and no free Form ID. Integration: the Blackwell word only at the bridge, record kinds above it, golden vectors, proposals. Our tree is the LPS p = 5 tree that SHD-CCP’s planned X(5, q) overlay is a quotient of.

Explainer 2

3 · A hyperbolic BERT, in two stages

Stage 1 is exact: pinned Unicode, WordPiece, a training-free address per WordNet synset, exact points in H³ and a new exact H² tree, exact ranking. Stage 2 is the best-supported route: a Euclidean encoder with the address as a hierarchy factor and a hyperbolic head. H² and H³ are factors, heads and views; Phase 5 built the views, the 2D and 3D pages.

Explainers 3, 13 and 14

4 · Every alternative has a role

Main routes, baselines, controls built to fail, later work and rejected ideas, each graded by its evidence. Fully hyperbolic encoders need a GPU; hyperbolic tokenization is an open gap; gradient-free training is for small heads; a Euclidean control at matched dimension is mandatory. Phase 4 ran that control for static embeddings: on the mammal closure, learned Lorentz beats learned Euclidean at d = 2 and 3 (closure MAP 0.88–0.97 against 0.16–0.23), and Euclidean is ahead at d = 50 (L6).

Explainers 5 and 12

5 · Language biochains: yes, as shards under a manifest

A biochain’s whole lineage is capped at 60 KB, so corpora become many unmodified shdccp/2 shards tied by an RFC 6962 manifest, with typed records, two privacy tiers and a hardened, attested importer that closes what verifyIntegrity misses. Phase 3 built it: 0 of 336 shard records differ from Owl Academy’s own (L8), and the verifier rejects all 450 registered tampers, four classes of which Owl’s verifyIntegrity accepts (L10).

Explainers 4 and 11

6 · A benchmark ladder across three tracks

Stage 0 determinism and the tokenizer; Stage 1 WordNet hierarchy (here, decided in Phases 1–4); Stage 2 lexical semantics (on the network widened in Phase 1; its claims, planned for Phase 4, are not registered yet); Stage 3 BabyLM and BLiMP; Stage 4 GLUE and MTEB (GPU). Browser, CPU and GPU tracks share one tokenizer record, data roots, seeds and metric code.

01

What this is

A new system, built the way Engram Mind’s Eye and Hyperbolic Oracles were: an analysis, measurement spikes, claims registered before any code that tests them, a Python reference, a browser core checked line for line against it, and pages that show the result.

Stage 1, the front end, takes text to exact, certified geometry. A pinned normaliser and WordPiece tokenizer give token ids. A training-free rule gives every WordNet noun synset a reduced word of the norm-5 quaternion tree as its address: ancestry becomes prefix order, and each word is an exact integer quaternion that right division unfolds. Each address becomes an exact point in H³ (Hyperbolic Oracles’ octahedral tree) and in H² (a new exact tree), and candidates are ranked by exact Lorentz products. Learned float64 embeddings, Lorentz against Euclidean at matched dimension, are snapped for certified storage. Corpus shards grow as unmodified SHD-CCP biochains under a new manifest.

Stage 2, the encoder, starts only after every Stage-1 claim is decided and reviewed: a small Euclidean BERT-style encoder that takes the address as a hierarchy factor, with a hyperbolic hierarchy head, on CPU at 1–10M parameters and then on a GPU.

The honest frame

Hyperbolic Oracles’ claim C1, “hyperbolic beats flat” for PolyStep optimisation, was refuted (0 supported, 12 refuted, 1 inconclusive of 13 cells). The literature finds clear gains from hyperbolic geometry on explicit hierarchies and modest or no gains on general language tasks. This system therefore bets on representation (trees in low dimension, where volume growth favours curvature) and on certification (exactness, invertibility, identical results in Python, Node and the browser), not on optimisation. Every hyperbolic claim has a matched Euclidean control, and encoder-level gains are predicted small or null at this scale.

Glossary

Address
A reduced word of the norm-5 quaternion tree given to a WordNet synset or a token by the L3 rule. Ancestry is prefix order; the word is an integer quaternion of norm 5length.
Norm-5 tree
The 6-regular tree generated by 1±2i, 1±2j, 1±2k (the Lubotzky–Phillips–Sarnak set for p = 5): 6 children at the root, 5 after.
Virtual letters
The extra letters a child needs when its parent has more than 5 children: max(1, ⌈log₅ c⌉) letters per child. They make siblings as close as parents (spike S3).
H², H³
The hyperbolic plane and 3-space, here as the hyperboloid model; the 2D and 3D systems.
Exact point
A point of the hyperboloid with rational coordinates; in the octahedral tree an integer vector over 7n.
Exact chart
How the pages draw: an isometry of H² or H³, computed exactly in BigInt, that moves the chosen centre to the origin; only the last step, to the disk or the ball on screen, is in floats. Every value the panels show is exact.
Graph-edge and closure MAP
Mean average precision when a node’s neighbours are its parent and children (graph-edge), or all its ancestors and descendants (closure). Ties count against the query.
N_B
The registered normaliser: BERT’s BasicTokenizer steps on a pinned Unicode 15.1 table, with byte spans, failing closed on code points unassigned in 15.1 (Amendment 1).
V0, V1
The two vocabularies of L1. V0 “lex0”: 8,192 WordPiece entries trained here on WordNet’s glosses and frozen by hash; it decides. V1: bert-base-uncased’s 30,522 entries, pinned at a Hugging Face revision; the second cell.
WordPiece
BERT’s subword tokenizer: greedy longest match, with “##” marking continuations.
Parity
The browser core regenerating the reference’s trace files line for line (tests/parity-lexicon.cjs); required before a claim decided in the reference is shown as holding in the browser.
Inversion
In L4(b), an adjacent pair of candidates whose exact keys are strictly increasing and whose float keys are strictly decreasing.
engram/1
Owl Academy’s 64-bit token key: the XOR of fixed letter grids, so only the parity of each letter’s count. Kept here as a checksum.
SHD-CCP
The Symbolic High Dimensional Context Compression Protocol: a 64-bit tile format and, in practice, the shdccp/2 stream of Owl Academy and BioChain-AI.
Blackwell word
The 64-bit layout every working SHD-CCP codec uses: form 63..60, parity 59, spin 58..56, quat32 55..24, payload16 23..8, freq 7..3, amp 2..0.
Biochain
A text of at most 60,000 bytes grown into a shdccp/2 stream with SHA-256 leaves, a Merkle root and a chiral weave, recreated losslessly.
RFC 6962 tree
The Certificate Transparency Merkle tree: domain-separated leaves and nodes, no duplication, inclusion and consistency proofs.
ATTEST/1
BioChain-AI’s signed commitment: ECDSA P-256 over “ATTEST/1|keyId|keyVector|SHA256(commitment)|epochToken”.
Records
The typed commitments above the word (D7), each a pipe-joined string hashed with SHA-256: LVOCAB/1 (the vocabulary and normaliser), LADDR/1 (the address table and its root), LPREP/1 (one shard), LCORPUS/1 (the manifest) and LGROW/1 (its growth), built in Phase 3; LBENCH/1 (one benchmark receipt per evaluated table: instance, arm, geometry, d, seed, rate, protocol, settings, table hashes and the exact MAPs’ hashes), built in Phase 4 (Amendment 10 item 12).
Snapped table
A learned float64 table rounded to the 2−32 grid: a Lorentz row through its Poincaré point by fence.js’s dyadic rule, a Euclidean row coordinate by coordinate. Its points are exact, so it is ranked exactly and named by a hash (L4(c); Amendment 10 item 6).
Exact weave
engram_core.chainQuaternion over the first 8 bytes of each leaf hash: an integer quaternion of norm 528K that unfolds, in order, to those prefixes (L9(b)). The order-sensitive summary proposed in place of the float chiral weave.
Self-consistent, authentic
The hardened verifier’s two verdicts (Explainer 4; Amendment 8 item 3). Self-consistent: every check but the signature passes, so the record, its derived layers and its place in the manifest agree. Authentic: the ATTEST/1 signature verifies against a registered, unrevoked key.
Tier S, Tier P
A Tier-S shard’s text travels in its stream; a Tier-P shard’s public LPREP/1 carries a salted root in place of its chainId, for text whose licence does not allow it to travel.
X(5, q)
The LPS Ramanujan graph: the norm-5 tree reduced mod a prime q, planned by SHD-CCP RFC 03 as an overlay.
Stage-1 gate
The review with you after Phase 5, and next now: no encoder code until every claim L1–L11 is decided (all eleven are, since Phase 4) and reviewed.
