Hyperbolic PolyStep

Gradient-free training and a multi-channel combinatorics solver in hyperbolic space. It takes polystep’s optimal-transport step, moves it onto the hyperboloid with the zero-point paper’s exact geometry, and runs three channels of it on the Zero-Point Tri-Engine’s clock. This page is the hub: every part of the project, how the parts connect, and where each is explained.

01

What this is

Three pieces meet here.

polystep

A gradient-free optimizer. Each step turns a polytope around the current parameters, scores its corners, weights them by optimal transport and moves to their weighted average. It trains spiking, quantized and hard-routed networks that have no useful gradient.

The zero-point paper

The exact geometry of hyperbolic space (the ghost map, the Klein model, boosts as complex rapidity), the eight-step clock and its 24-frame schedule, the 720-frame pump, and the limits of what any of it can decide.

The tri-engine pages

Three engines on one clock, pairing at the zero point, a timing clutch and membrane, and memory on the outer and inner manifolds. They become three solver channels.

Put together: polystep’s step on the hyperboloid, with every candidate an exact rational point, run by three channels that each take one step per joint cycle and pair their certified answers at the zero point. It trains small hard-threshold networks and attacks combinatorial problems (3-colouring, MAX-SAT, tree addresses) without gradients.

What it will not show

A heuristic solver is the paper’s Helix A: it proposes, and an exact certifier checks. Its successes or failures say nothing about P versus NP. The paper’s Theorem 5.11 makes a general solver of this kind equivalent to P = NP only if it is an integrable encoding of SAT, which is open. Proposition 5.13 makes black-box search cost the whole volume of the tree, for any method. The boundary chart lays out what the experiments can and cannot decide.

07

Glossary

Hyperboloid
The points with \(-t^2 + |x|^2 = -1,\ t > 0\): a model of hyperbolic space in which isometries are Lorentz matrices.
Poincaré ball
Hyperbolic space drawn in the unit ball, angles true, geodesics circular arcs. Used for pictures.
Klein model
The same space in the unit ball with straight geodesics. Used for the centroid, which becomes a convex combination.
Ghost map
The paper’s rational map from the ball to the hyperboloid, \(G(p) = ((1+|p|^2),\,2p)/(1-|p|^2)\).
Particle
One candidate solution being improved (a point, or a vector of parameters).
Polytope, vertices, probes
The candidate set around a particle: corners of a rotated orthoplex at the step radius, and probe points along each direction.
ε (temperature)
How sharply the weights favour low-cost vertices: small ε picks the best corner, large ε averages.
Channel
One of three particle populations, on one of the three 120° branches of the clock.
Packet
The 12 frames from a branch’s onload to its offload; it carries one solver step.
Helix A / Helix B
The proposer (the solver) and the exact certifier, as in the paper’s machine model.
Register
720 frames; at its end the pump evaluates the holonomy of each channel’s exact trajectory.
