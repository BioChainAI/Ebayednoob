Six answers

1

What we built is exact geometry, not language learning

Engram Mind’s Eye and Hyperbolic Oracles give exact quaternion addresses, an exact tree in H³ to depth 28, hyperboloid tools, Merkle chains, an outlier fence and a method that checks browser code against Python line for line. Before Phase 1, none of the four repositories had a subword tokenizer, a vocabulary or a language benchmark; none had learned embeddings until Phase 4 trained static ones, and none has an encoder. The engram/1 key keeps only the parity of each letter’s count, so LISTEN = SILENT and every number lands on the zero key: it cannot be a token id. PolyStep is gradient-free and trained 48 parameters at most; its “hyperbolic beats flat” claim was refuted.

2

SHD-CCP is three layers that disagree, and one bridge

A specification repository (RFC 01 tile, a Kernel Ontology, golden vectors; no chain format), the working Blackwell word and shdccp/2 stream in Owl Academy and BioChain-AI, and prose that was never built. At least eight 64-bit layouts exist: the check word 9841D88D8B003CEA reads as Form 9, 8 or 13 depending on the reader, and every Form value is already claimed. Integration means the Blackwell word only where we hand data to Owl Academy and BioChain-AI, record kinds above the word, golden vectors, and proposals instead of edits. The strongest link is mathematical: our tree’s generators 1±2i, 1±2j, 1±2k are the Lubotzky–Phillips–Sarnak set for p = 5, so SHD-CCP’s planned X(5, q) overlay is a quotient of the tree our addresses already live on.

3

A hyperbolic BERT, in two stages

Stage 1 is exact and needs no training: pinned Unicode, WordPiece, a training-free address for every WordNet synset in the quaternion tree, exact points in H³ and in a new exact H² tree, exact ranking. Stage 2 follows the best-supported route in the literature: a Euclidean encoder that takes the address as a hierarchy factor, with a hyperbolic hierarchy head. H² and H³ are far below language-model width, so they serve as factors, heads and views, not as the main embedding.

4

Every alternative has a role

Main routes, baselines, controls built to fail, later work and rejected ideas. Fully hyperbolic encoders (HyboNet, HyperCore, HELM) need a GPU. No published work applies hyperbolic geometry at the tokenizer level, an open gap. Gradient-free methods fine-tune heads; they do not pretrain. A Euclidean control at matched dimension is mandatory: one study found flat embeddings catching up from about 50 dimensions.

5

Language biochains: yes, as shards under a manifest

A biochain’s whole lineage is capped at 60 KB, because each growth step regrows all its text. So a corpus becomes many unmodified shdccp/2 shards, each one a biochain Owl Academy already accepts, tied together by an append-only RFC 6962 manifest, with typed signed records, two privacy tiers, and a stricter importer that closes what Owl’s verifier misses.

6

A benchmark ladder, across three compute tracks

Stage 0 checks determinism and the tokenizer, Stage 1 WordNet hierarchy reconstruction, Stage 2 lexical semantics, Stage 3 small-scale pretraining (BabyLM, BLiMP), Stage 4 sentence tasks (GLUE, MTEB). Browser, CPU and GPU tracks share one tokenizer record, the data roots, the seeds and the metric code, so their results compare.

The address rule

Each WordNet noun synset’s spanning parent is its first hypernym pointer. A child of a node with c children takes max(1, ⌈log₅ c⌉) letters of the tree, so ancestry becomes prefix order and every address is an exact integer quaternion that right division unfolds. Nodes with more than five children need extra “virtual” letters, and those put siblings as close as parents: on WordNet’s verb forest the exact tree’s graph-edge MAP is 0.6309 (spike S3), against 0.9974 on edges that need one letter.
