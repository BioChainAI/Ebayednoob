Segments A and B, bound by the coupler a binding check only

The binding B = q₂q₁ after each token’s twist, in BERT order (outline: the token’s segment)

Engram Mind’s Eye’s coupler (claim E5’s law): each token’s word quaternion w twists both structures, q₁ ← w q₁ and q₂ ← q₂ w̄, and the binding q₂q₁ is checked exactly after every twist. This shows that the two segments’ structures were updated as mirror images in exact arithmetic. It is a binding check only: not a feature, not a security mechanism, and it cannot tell which tokens were in A or B. Integrity is carried by SHA-256 records (the shard panel).
