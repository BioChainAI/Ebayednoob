Two Structures, One Coupler

Six calculations in three-dimensional hyperbolic space. Structure 1 and structure 2 each run engines A, B and C on one 24-frame clock, each on its own instance with its own memory chain. Every move turns a particle’s frame by a twist. The coupler applies that twist to one structure and its inverse to the other’s mirrored frame, both ways, so the binding between paired particles never changes. It checks this exactly at every zero point and keeps a third memory chain. Steer lets the frames turn each polytope; carry only transports them, and the six calculations are then bit for bit what they would be without a coupler. Keys: Space plays or pauses, . advances a frame, z jumps to the next zero point. Manual and proofs · The 3D decoder · Hub
