Coupled Oscillator Field

Sample module — a placeholder demonstrating this hub's format. More simulations will be added here over time.

Each node is a damped oscillator connected to its two neighbors on a ring. Nudging one node (click the field) injects energy that propagates around the ring according to the coupling strength k — a minimal analogue of the standing-wave and phase-synchronization behavior explored elsewhere on this site (cymatics, Kuramoto-style entrainment). Click anywhere in the field to perturb the nearest oscillator.
