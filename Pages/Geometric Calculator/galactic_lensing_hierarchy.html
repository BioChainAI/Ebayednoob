  • Data. Constants, the seven pulsars and every computed number come from the_galactic_lensing_hierarchy.md and verify_galactic_hierarchy.py. "Run Verification" is a line-by-line port of the script, including Python's Mersenne Twister seeded with 20261026, so the exact-arithmetic checks run on the same random cases; its output is compared with the script's captured output.
  • Simulated observations. The "model" side of each channel is a forward prediction from simple mass models: Sgr A* as a point mass; the nuclear star cluster as a Hernquist sphere of 2.5 × 10⁷ M☉ with a 4.2 pc half-mass radius HEURISTIC; and System III's observer terms (Gaia drift, Gaia acceleration, solar reflex). The "data" side is model + Gaussian noise + any injected signal. Pulsar position angles on the sky are illustrative; only projected distances are from the table. Line-of-sight depths are unknown (Problem G1) and are set equal to the projected offset, as in §6.2.
  • Clock. One master clock: pulseCount = ⌊t·60⌋, gears at 2π·n/720, −2π·n/72·η and 2π·n/10·0.1 with n = 12·pulseCount mod 720 (engine v10). One 720-frame cycle is one observation epoch of 0.25 yr. 1× speed advances 0.5 cycle per second; physics uses a fixed 1/60 s step with interpolated rendering.
