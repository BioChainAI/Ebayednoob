Telemetry log

  • What is reversed. Part 2 analyses: one measured total per class is split into three systems' models and the residual, the emergent fourth. This page composes: three independent results go in, and their exact total is the fourth. In both, the four make a group whose any one member is fixed by the other three (Proposition G1). Part 2's lock means the three engines agree about the data; here it means the three calculations agree with each other.
  • One clock, three schedules. All three engines run on engine v10's master clock (pulse p, node k = p mod 30). A job may be submitted at any moment, but it waits on the outer manifold until the wheel reaches node 0. It then spends one full wheel turn on each tier: T4 ingest, T3 compile, T2 and T1 evaluate, T0 reduce and latch, landing on node 29 after 150 pulses. Jobs started on different turns are at different depths, yet every engine always samples the same node, so their results can be combined with no clock conversion. A job that lands first is held in memory until the others arrive DEFINITION.
  • Quadrance out. On convergence the page reads out, with no further input, the Triple-Quad Formula of the three landing nodes: three quadrances, the quadrea in two exact forms, the ratio χ and the exact closure P₄*. The triangle encodes agreement, not the values themselves. Values are kept exactly in memory, which is why the readout and the memory are separate.
  • Memory on both manifolds. A, B, C are written to the outer manifolds and Y to the inner manifold, at the word's address on the wheel. Erase any one cell of a word (click it) and it is rebuilt from the other three. "Alter one cell" changes a stored value by 2⁻²⁴ without updating the others. Parity then detects it, the Merkle chain locates it (the deepest layer that differs is the altered leaf), and the parity rebuilds it.
  • Timing clutch. Each engine has its own clock, the pulses during which its clutch is engaged, and its helices turn only then. With hold finished engines an engine that has latched disengages: its gear stops and its result stays where it landed while the others catch up. With free-run every engine keeps turning, so a held result rides its helix away from where it landed. The engines counter-rotate, so equal results then land on a sheared triangle: the asynchronous frustration. With free-run + membrane the three carried spins split as in Proposition 7.14 into a common rotation s, which leaves the three congruent, and an orthogonal differential z, which is the shear. The membrane takes z in at the step-2 window and removes it exactly at the step-6 window, 180° of spinor phase later, where the three pair DEFINITION.
